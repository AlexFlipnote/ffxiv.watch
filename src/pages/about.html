About ffxiv.watch

A fan site made for the phone in your hand while you play, with no account, no ads and no trackers. Most pages load in under 150 KB, and not one of the 3,300+ pages goes over 1 MB, even scrolled all the way down. Your settings and progress stay in your browser.

Tested on

Real devices, from a budget Samsung Galaxy A40 to a high-end desktop PC, with an iPhone 14 and newer and a Google Pixel 10 Pro XL in between, in Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari and Samsung Internet. The A40 is the one it's tuned on, so it stays smooth on older and cheaper phones too.

Where the data comes from

  • The game's own data, datamined by XIVAPI's ffxiv-datamining: the nodes, fish, vistas, zones and weather rates, with the maps and icons from XIVAPI.
  • When each fish bites, its bait and intuition, from Carbuncle Plushy's fish tracker.
  • The other fish's bait, hookset and tug, blue fish times and hidden node items, from Teamcraft.
  • Maintenance and events, from the official Lodestone.
  • The PvP map rotations, from PvP Calendar.

The countdowns and weather forecasts are worked out from the game's own rules, the same way the game does it, so they don't wait on anyone reporting them. How often something is up, and the longest wait for it, comes from a year of those forecasts.

How often it's updated

The Lodestone is checked every hour, so new maintenance and events show up on their own. The game data is updated after each patch, once the datamined files have it.
